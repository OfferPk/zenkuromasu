Rules of Kuromasu (黒どこ)

🌸 The Goal

Discover which cells contain hidden Black Stones (●) and which are White Paths (·), guided by the numbered circle clues.

1. Line of Sight

Each circled number indicates the total number of white cells visible in a straight line horizontally and vertically from that clue (including the clue cell itself). Black stones block vision.

2. No Adjacent Black Stones

Two black stones never touch orthogonally (they cannot share an edge). Diagonal touching is permitted.

3. Continuous White Connectivity

All white cells must remain seamlessly connected in a single group. No white cell or group may be isolated or trapped by black stones.

💡 Controls & Tips

• Tap a cell to cycle: Empty → Black Stone → White Dot → Empty.

• Use the Stone or Dot tool buttons for rapid one-tap placement on mobile.

• Number clues turn Gold when their line of sight is perfectly satisfied.
