## Full Description (4,000 characters max)
Immerse yourself in the tranquil quietude of a traditional Japanese rock and ink garden with ZenKuromasu, a serene logic puzzle inspired by the beloved Nikoli classic Kuromasu (黒どこ / Kurodoko, meaning "Where are the black cells?").

In ZenKuromasu, your goal is to uncover the harmonious placement of black sumi ink stones and open white pebble pathways on peaceful paper grids. Guided only by numbers in circles, you follow the pure geometry of light and line-of-sight to bring every board into perfect balance.

=== THE RULES OF KUROMASU ===
1. Line of Sight Clues: Each circled number indicates the exact total count of white (unshaded) cells visible in horizontal and vertical straight lines from that clue, including the clue itself.
2. Obstacles Block Sight: Black stones block lines of sight in all four directions. Clue cells can never be shaded black.
3. No Adjacent Black Stones: Two black stones can never touch orthogonally (they cannot share an edge). Diagonal contact is permitted.
4. Continuous White Paths: All white cells must form a single, contiguous orthogonal group across the board. No white cell or region may be isolated.
